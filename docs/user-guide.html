M&M Austin Limousine
Staff User Guide

Set up your cars and prices first, then surcharges, then handle bookings. Sign in to the dashboard at app.austinlimoshuttle.com/dashboard.

1Set up your cars & rates

Start here. Each car has a rate card that prices every trip automatically.
1
Menu → Vehicles & rates. You see every car, its seats, hourly rate and minimum fare.
2
Click a car to edit its rate card — the per-mile bands, plus hourly rate, minimum fare and meet & greet fee.
CARE
Bands must start at 0 and be continuous. Leave the last band’s upper limit empty for “and onwards.” A gap prices those miles at $0; an overlap charges twice.

3Bookings come in & assign a driver

A customer books online; it lands here for you to handle.
Overview — what needs attention

The red “No driver” tile counts bookings still to assign.
1
Bookings (or Dispatch) → click the blue reference.
2
Open the Driver dropdown and choose a driver.
3
Click Save changes — the customer is emailed automatically.
NOTE
Drivers aren’t tied to cars. Any driver can take any booking — the car was already chosen by the customer.
